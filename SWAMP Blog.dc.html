Sep 7, 2024 · First Aid

Is Your Smartphone Medical ID Enabled?

Old school medical ID bracelets are still a surefire way to ensure first responders are alerted to your medical conditions in case you can't speak for yourself.

Both iOS and Android let you fill out an emergency Medical ID accessible right from the lock screen, no passcode needed. Add allergies, conditions, and an emergency contact before your next ride.
